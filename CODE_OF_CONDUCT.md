# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes welcoming language, respect for different viewpoints, constructive feedback, focus on the community's interests and empathy toward others.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may clarify standards and take fair corrective action. They may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for threatening, offensive or harmful behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to [app.support@company.example](mailto:app.support@company.example). Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled with an appropriate response.
